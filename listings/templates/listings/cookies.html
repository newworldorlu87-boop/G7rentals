Cookies Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences, improve performance, and provide personalized experiences. Cookies do not harm your device and cannot access other files on your computer.

2. How We Use Cookies

G7Rentals uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the platform to function, including authentication, security, and session management.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings such as language, location filters, and display preferences.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our platform so we can improve it.
  • Marketing cookies: Used to deliver relevant property recommendations and measure advertising effectiveness.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
sessionid Maintains your login session 2 weeks
csrftoken Security — prevents cross-site request forgery 1 year
g7_preferences Stores your filter and display preferences 6 months
_ga, _gid Google Analytics — usage statistics 24 hours – 2 years

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may allow trusted third parties to place cookies for analytics and advertising. These include Google Analytics for usage insights and social media platforms for sharing features. Third-party cookies are subject to the respective privacy policies of those providers.

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage cookies

Note: Disabling essential cookies may prevent you from logging in or using core features of G7Rentals.

6. Cookie Consent

When you first visit G7Rentals, we display a cookie consent banner. By clicking "Accept All" or continuing to use the site, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy. You can withdraw consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings or contacting us.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy to reflect changes in technology or legal requirements. Significant changes will be notified via email or platform announcement. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact us at privacy@g7rentals.com or visit our Privacy Policy for more information.
